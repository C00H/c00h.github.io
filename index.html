Hi, I'm Shuoqi Li. I'm an M.S. student in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, expecting to graduate in 2028. I graduated from The Chinese University of Hong Kong with a B.S. in Computational Data Science and First Class Honours in 2026.

Research & Projects

ComUICoder & ComUIBench

Jun 2025 – Jul 2026 Research Assistant · CUHK Faculty of Engineering · Supervised by Prof. Michael R. Lyu

I refined screenshot segmentation using UIED detections, implemented graph matching for reusable UI blocks, and built element-level feedback for generated pages. For ComUIBench, I collected related subpages across 40 websites (150 pages) and helped evaluate the system. Project code

Federated recommendation

Jun – Aug 2024 Summer Research Intern · CUHK Faculty of Engineering · Supervised by Prof. Irwin King

I reproduced recommendation and federated learning models, combined them in a setup that kept user data local, and evaluated the models on public datasets.
